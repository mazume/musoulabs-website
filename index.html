Musou is an independent software studio in Chiba, Japan. We make small, carefully built apps for iOS, and we take the time to get them right.

About

We started Musou to make a place for creation — somewhere to work with a clear mind, begin from nothing, and let each product take the time it needs.

We believe people are happiest with the things they love, and that this is as true of an app as of anything else. So we try to build products with a taste of their own: the kind that comes from a studio's philosophy and accumulated work, and cannot be copied.

The name Musou carries three meanings at once.

無創to create from nothing
無双without equal
夢想to dream
Products

Kairos

In development — coming to the App Store

Alarms and timers, treated as one thing: a moment you want to be told about. Kairos schedules them through AlarmKit, so they ring reliably on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island even when the app is closed, and lays them out on a monthly calendar so you can see your month at a glance.

Run several timers at once, group them by what they're for, and set alarms that repeat weekly, monthly, on the second Thursday, or once a year. Everything stays on your device. No account, no server.
